How our pricing works

The number you approve is the number you build

We issue itemized pricing after the design phase. Once you approve it, the price is fixed, with no hidden fees. It starts with a free consultation and no obligation, moves into a design track, and only then reaches demolition.

Free consultation

A no-obligation planning conversation about your home, your goals, and a realistic budget before any commitment.

Design track

We design in 2D or 3D before any demolition. 3D rendering is available at $1 per square foot so you see the result before you build it.

Itemized, fixed price

You get a line-by-line price. Approve it and it is locked, so there are no surprise fees between design and handover.

This is the same fixed-price approach behind every engagement, from a home remodel to a ground-up custom home or an ADU or home addition.

Frequently Asked Questions

Pricing & warranty, answered

Is a design-build firm more expensive?

Usually not. One team avoids the duplicated roles and the change orders that come from handoffs between a separate architect, designer, and builder. Our pricing is itemized and locked after design, so you are not paying for the gaps between three companies.

Will my remodel go over budget?

Your itemized price is locked once you approve it after design. We hold a 10 to 20 percent contingency for the unknowns that walls reveal, and one accountable team controls the scope, so there is no finger-pointing between trades when something needs a decision.

How does your pricing work?

It starts with a free consultation, then a design phase, then an itemized price you approve. Once you approve it, the price is fixed. There are no hidden fees - the number you approve is the number you build.

What warranty do you offer?

A 2-year cosmetic workmanship warranty and a 10-year structural warranty on foundation and framing. UniqHaus is a licensed, bonded, and insured California builder, CSLB #1158697.

What is the smallest project you take?

We take projects from $50,000 and up, from focused kitchen, bath, and interior transformations to whole-house remodels and custom homes.

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. The initial consultation is free and carries no obligation - it is a planning conversation, not a sales pitch.
