How Long Does a Kitchen Remodel Take in San Jose? (2026 Timeline)

A full kitchen remodel in San Jose takes about 12-20 weeks: 6-12 weeks of design, permits and ordering, then 6-10 weeks of construction.

If you are planning a kitchen remodel in San Jose, the question that matters most for your daily life is simple: how long will my kitchen be out of commission? The honest answer is that most of the schedule is decided before a single cabinet comes down. Below is a realistic, phase-by-phase 2026 timeline for Santa Clara County homeowners, plus the specific things that stretch a 12-week project into a 20-week one and how to avoid them.

How long does a kitchen remodel take in San Jose?

A full kitchen remodel in San Jose takes about 12 to 20 weeks from first design meeting to final inspection. That breaks into roughly 6 to 12 weeks of design, permitting, and material ordering followed by 6 to 10 weeks of active construction. A light cosmetic refresh (new counters, backsplash, paint, hardware, no layout change) can wrap in 4 to 6 weeks. At the other end, a project with full-custom cabinetry and a moved wall can run 20 to 28 weeks. The construction phase is shorter than most homeowners expect; it is the ordering and approvals that dominate the calendar.

The key insight: your kitchen is only unusable during construction (6-10 weeks). The design and permitting months happen while you keep cooking. Front-loading decisions and orders during that window is the single best way to shorten the disruptive part.

What are the phases of a kitchen remodel timeline?

A San Jose kitchen remodel moves through seven phases, and several overlap. Cabinet fabrication runs in the background while permits are reviewed, which is why ordering early matters so much. Here is the full 2026 schedule:

Notice that cabinet lead time overlaps permit review. A well-run kitchen remodel orders cabinets the day the design is locked, so the 4-8 week fabrication runs at the same time as the 2-4 week permit review instead of after it. That overlap alone can save a month.

How long does the design and planning phase take?

Design and planning takes 2 to 4 weeks for a straightforward kitchen, and 3 to 6 weeks when you are changing the layout, relocating plumbing, or opening a wall. This is where you finalize the floor plan, select cabinets, counters, tile, appliances, and fixtures, and produce the drawings the city needs for permits. Because UniqHaus handles architecture, interior design, and construction under one roof, selections and the permit set move in parallel rather than being handed between separate firms.

The slowest thing in this phase is decision-making, not drafting. Kitchens with 40-plus individual selections stall when homeowners take weeks to pick a faucet finish. Lock your major selections early: cabinets first (they have the longest lead time), then countertops, then everything else.

How long do kitchen remodel permits take in San Jose?

Kitchen permits in San Jose take 2 to 4 weeks for a straightforward remodel and 8 to 12 weeks for major projects that require full plan review (structural work, moved walls, or significant electrical and plumbing changes). Simple electrical or plumbing permits often clear in 1 to 2 weeks, standard residential plan review runs about 10 to 15 business days, and the city's Express Plan Check returns comments in 2 to 3 days for an added fee. Note that San Jose has reportedly been running about 2 to 3 weeks behind its published estimates in 2026, so build in a buffer.

You need a permit whenever the work touches electrical, plumbing, mechanical, or structure. A true like-for-like cabinet swap with no wiring, plumbing, or wall changes often does not. Typical kitchen permit fees run $1,500 to $3,000 on a $50K remodel (roughly 3-10% of declared construction value), based on the City of San José FY2025-26 hourly rates of $227 for issuance, $325 for plan review, and $315 for inspection. A 2026 wrinkle: the 2025 California Building Codes and 2025 Title 24 Part 6 Energy Code took effect January 1, 2026, so permitted kitchen alterations now trigger higher efficiency requirements for lighting, insulation, and windows. We fold all of this into our San Jose kitchen remodel permits handling so the review clock starts the moment your plans are ready.

Why do custom cabinets and countertops take so long?

Cabinets are the timeline's pacemaker: semi-custom cabinets take 4 to 8 weeks to fabricate and ship, and full-custom cabinetry takes 8 to 12+ weeks. They are built to order in a shop, not pulled from a warehouse, so the clock starts the day you place the order, not the day demo finishes. This is exactly why we order cabinets during design. Wait until after demo and you add 2 to 3 months of an unusable kitchen.

Countertops add a second sequencing constraint. A fabricator cannot template your quartz or stone until the cabinets are physically installed and level, because the template is a precise map of the actual boxes. After templating, fabrication and installation take 1 to 2 weeks. Quartz, the most popular Bay Area choice at $80-$150 per square foot installed, means you live with a countertop-less kitchen for about two weeks mid-project no matter how well you plan. That gap is normal and expected.

What causes kitchen remodel delays, and how do you avoid them?

Most delays trace to four causes, and three of the four are preventable with early decisions. In order of impact:

  • Custom cabinet lead time (6-12+ weeks). Avoid it by ordering before demo, during design, so fabrication overlaps permitting.
  • Permit and plan-review backlog on layout-change projects. Avoid it by submitting a complete, code-current plan set and using Express Plan Check when the schedule is tight.
  • Change orders and slow selections. Every mid-build change ripples through ordering and trades. Avoid it by finalizing all selections before construction starts.
  • Hidden conditions such as old wiring, corroded plumbing, or non-code framing found at demo. You cannot prevent these, so keep a 10-20% contingency and a buffer week or two in the schedule.

A general remodel of adjacent spaces adds coordination too; if your kitchen ties into a broader home remodeling project, sequencing the trades across rooms is what keeps the whole job on schedule.

How much does a kitchen remodel cost in San Jose in 2026?

A San Jose kitchen remodel costs $8,000-$18,000 for a cosmetic update, $55,000-$85,000 for a mid-range remodel (semi-custom cabinets, quartz, tile backsplash, new appliances, no layout change on a 150-200 sq ft kitchen), and $90,000-$140,000+ for an upscale project with a new layout, custom cabinetry, and premium finishes. On a per-square-foot basis, expect $225-$425, with San Jose running roughly 20-35% above national cost-guide averages because of labor, permits, and Bay Area finish expectations. Cabinets alone eat 30-40% of the budget. For a full breakdown, see our companion guide on how much a kitchen remodel costs in San Jose.

Is a kitchen remodel worth it?

Yes, financially and practically. Bay Area kitchen remodels return about 65-80% of their cost at resale, the highest ROI of any home improvement. Minor updates under $30K recover 75-85%, while upscale projects over $90K return 55-70%. In San Jose's competitive inventory, a move-in-ready kitchen also drives faster sales and offers at or above list. Beyond resale, you get 15-plus years of daily use out of a space you live in every morning, which is the return most homeowners actually feel.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a kitchen remodel take in San Jose?

About 12 to 20 weeks start to finish: roughly 6 to 12 weeks of design, permits, and material ordering, then 6 to 10 weeks of active construction. A cosmetic refresh can finish in 4 to 6 weeks, while a full-custom, layout-change kitchen can run 20 to 28 weeks.

What is the most time-consuming part of a kitchen remodel?

Cabinet lead time. Semi-custom cabinets take 4 to 8 weeks to fabricate and full-custom takes 8 to 12+ weeks. Because they are built to order, ordering them early during design, so fabrication overlaps permit review, is the single biggest way to shorten the project.

How long do kitchen remodel permits take in San Jose?

Straightforward kitchen permits take 2 to 4 weeks, and major projects needing full plan review take 8 to 12 weeks. San Jose's Express Plan Check returns comments in 2 to 3 days for an added fee. The city has been running about 2 to 3 weeks behind published estimates in 2026, so build in a buffer.

Do I need a permit to remodel my kitchen in San Jose?

Yes, whenever the work involves electrical, plumbing, mechanical, or structural or layout changes. A true like-for-like cabinet swap with no wiring, plumbing, or wall changes often does not require one. Permits are filed at SJPermits.org and typically cost $1,500 to $3,000 on a $50K remodel.

How long do custom cabinets take to arrive?

Semi-custom cabinets take 4 to 8 weeks and full-custom cabinets take 8 to 12+ weeks because they are manufactured to order. The clock starts when you place the order, not when demo finishes, so ordering during the design phase saves 2 to 3 months of an unusable kitchen.

Can you live in your house during a kitchen remodel?

Usually yes. Your kitchen is only unusable during the 6 to 10 weeks of active construction, and the counter-free gap during fabrication lasts about 2 weeks. Most homeowners set up a temporary kitchenette with a microwave, fridge, and hot plate and stay in the home throughout.

Key Takeaways

  • A full kitchen remodel in San Jose takes about 12-20 weeks: 6-12 weeks of design, permits and ordering, then 6-10 weeks of construction.
  • Cabinet lead time is the biggest variable: semi-custom adds 4-8 weeks, full-custom 8-12+ weeks and can push the whole project to 20-28 weeks.
  • Demo is the fastest phase (2-5 days); permits (2-4 weeks, up to 8-12 for layout changes) and countertop fabrication set the real clock.
  • Order cabinets during design, not after demo, to save 2-3 months of dead time on site.
