The challenge

The owners needed significantly more space on their existing San Jose lot, and preferred to expand and rebuild rather than move.

Our approach

UniqHaus delivered a major addition and whole-home remodel of the Sakhalkar Residence, expanding it to four bedrooms, three bathrooms, and roughly 2,207 sq ft of living space, plus a porch, patio, and attached garage on the 7,405 sq ft lot.

The result

A rebuilt, expanded home completed in July 2021, four beds and three baths of modern living space, letting the family stay on their lot with the room they needed.
